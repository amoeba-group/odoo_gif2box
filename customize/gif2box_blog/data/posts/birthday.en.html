A first birthday is a real milestone: the baby sits steadily, is starting to walk and wants to do everything alone, eating included. That is why the most meaningful first-birthday gifts are the ones that make each meal more independent for the baby, and less to clean up for the parents.

What does a one-year-old need?

By now most babies eat a wider range of foods and love to grab things. The most useful items are:

  • Suction bowls and plates: they grip the table, so a swipe is unlikely to tip them over.
  • A spoon and fork sized for small hands: for learning to feed themselves.
  • A training cup: the step from bottle to cup, with a straw or a spill-proof lid.
  • Small food containers: for meals on outings or at nursery.

First-birthday gifts parents love

A silicone suction bowl

Soft silicone does not break, washes easily, and the suction base keeps the bowl in place. Parents use it at nearly every meal.

A self-feeding set

A soft-tipped spoon and fork with chunky handles help babies coordinate hand and mouth. A set with its own case is easy to take along.

A divided plate and a training cup

A divided plate lets parents serve several foods at once, and a training cup helps the baby get used to drinking on their own.

Tips for a first-birthday gift

  • Pick bright colours and friendly animal shapes: babies are keener to eat from them.
  • Check for safe materials, such as BPA-free platinum silicone.
  • A coordinated set of several pieces looks generous and is more useful than a single item.
